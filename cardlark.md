# Privacy Policy

**Last updated:** November 6, 2026

## Introduction

Cardlark ("the App") respects your privacy and is committed to protecting your personal information. This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you use the App.

## Information We Collect

Cardlark is designed to collect no personal information at all. The App has no backend server and no user accounts.

The App stores the following information locally on your device only:

* Flashcard decks, including card text, images, audio, and tags you create
* Study and review history, used to schedule future reviews
* Target dates and folder organization you set
* Reminder and notification preferences
* App settings and preferences

None of this data ever leaves your device unless you explicitly choose to share or transfer it (see below).

## Local WiFi Deck Transfer

Cardlark lets you send a deck directly to a nearby device over your local WiFi network, using Apple's MultipeerConnectivity framework. This transfer:

* Is encrypted end-to-end
* Only works between devices on the same local network
* Never passes through any server operated by the developer or anyone else
* Only happens when you explicitly choose to send or receive a deck

## Deck Export and Sharing

You can export a deck as a file and share it yourself, however you choose (AirDrop, WhatsApp, email, etc.). This is entirely under your control — the developer has no visibility into decks you export or share.

## Analytics and Tracking

Cardlark does not use any analytics, crash reporting, or third-party advertising networks. The App does not collect usage data, does not sell your personal data, and does not track you across other apps or websites.

## Notifications

If you choose to enable the daily study reminder, the App schedules a local notification on your device. This reminder does not reveal how many cards are due or any other information about your decks — it only reminds you to open the App.

Notification preferences can be changed at any time in your device settings.

## Data Security

Your data is stored locally on your device using Apple-provided technologies and protections. While reasonable measures are taken to protect your information, no method of electronic storage is completely secure.

## Children's Privacy

Cardlark is not directed toward children under the age of 13 and does not knowingly collect personal information from children — in fact, it does not collect personal information from anyone, since it has no backend to collect information to.

## Your Rights

Since all your data stays on your device, you are always in full control of it. You may delete your data at any time by:

* Deleting individual cards or decks within the App
* Deleting the App from your device, which removes all of its data

## Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time. Any updates will be reflected by revising the "Last updated" date above.

## Contact

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, you can contact the developer through the App Store listing or support contact provided with the App.

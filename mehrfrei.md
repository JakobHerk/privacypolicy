Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 11, 2026

Introduction

Mehrfrei – Urlaubsplaner ("the App") respects your privacy and is committed to protecting your personal information. This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you use the App.

Information We Collect

Mehrfrei is designed to work without an account and to collect as little personal information as possible. The App does not require you to sign up or provide your name, email address or any other contact details.

The App stores the following information locally on your device only:

- Your country and region (federal state or canton)
- Your working days and your annual number of vacation days
- The vacation days you have planned, and any carried-over days
- Optional settings such as enabled optional holidays, a change of working model during the year, and whether school holidays are shown
- A plan you have imported from a partner (see "Shared Plans")

Public holidays and school holidays are calculated from data bundled with the App. No internet connection is needed for this, and no request is sent to any server for it.

No Cloud, No Account

Your data is not synced to iCloud or to any server operated by the developer. The developer has no access to your plans. The App's widget reads the same locally stored data on your device.

In-App Purchase (Mehrfrei Plus)

Mehrfrei Plus is a one-time purchase processed by Apple through the App Store. To validate purchases and restore them on your devices, the App uses RevenueCat, a third-party purchase management service. For this purpose, RevenueCat receives an anonymous, randomly generated user identifier created by the App, purchase and transaction information provided by Apple (such as product identifier, purchase date and transaction identifier), and basic device information such as the device model, operating system version, App version and language. This data is used solely to confirm that you own Mehrfrei Plus. It is not linked to your name or email address, and your planning data is never sent to RevenueCat.

For details, see RevenueCat's privacy policy at https://www.revenuecat.com/privacy and Apple's privacy policy for App Store purchases.

Calendar Access (optional)

If you use the "Export to calendar" feature, the App asks for permission to access your calendar. The App uses this permission only to create all-day events for your planned free blocks and to replace events it created itself on a later export. The App does not read, analyse or transmit any other calendar entries. You can revoke this permission at any time in the iOS Settings.

Shared Plans (optional)

You can export your plan as a file (.mehrfrei) and share it with a partner, for example via Messages or email. This file contains your country, region, working days, number of vacation days and your planned vacation days. Sharing is always initiated by you, uses the sharing method you choose, and the file is sent directly to the recipient – it does not pass through servers of the developer. A plan you import from a partner is stored locally on your device and can be removed at any time in the App's settings.

Analytics and Tracking

Mehrfrei does not use analytics services or third-party advertising networks. The App does not sell your personal data and does not track you across other apps or websites.

Data Security

Your data is stored on your device using Apple-provided technologies and protections. While reasonable measures are taken to protect your information, no method of electronic storage is completely
secure.

Children's Privacy

Mehrfrei is not directed toward children under the age of 13 and does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

- Using "Alle Daten löschen" in the App's settings
- Removing an imported partner plan in the App's settings
- Deleting the App from your device
